Stack Rush Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Stack Rush is a browser-based stacking game. We designed it to work without accounts or server logins.

Data stored on your device

The game may save the following locally in your browser or app storage:

  • High scores and mode bests
  • Career progress, achievements, and settings (sound, haptics, city theme)
  • Your display name for weekly score sharing
  • Weekly board entries you add or import from share links

This data stays on your device unless you clear app/site data or uninstall.

Data we do not collect

  • No account registration
  • No email or phone collection by the game itself
  • No location tracking
  • No advertising identifiers

Audio

Sound and music play from files bundled with the game. No voice recording is used.

Share links

Weekly mode can generate links containing a player name and score. Only share links if you are comfortable sharing that information.

Hosting

The game is delivered over HTTPS. Your browser or the Android app loads game files from the publisher's web host.

Children

Stack Rush is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

This policy may be updated when features change. Continued use of the game means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about privacy: contact the developer through the support channel listed on the Google Play store listing.
