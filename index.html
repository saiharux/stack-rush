How to Play

A block slides across the top. Tap to drop it onto the tower.

Line it up perfectly for big bonuses and combos. Miss the edge and your block gets sliced off. Miss completely — game over.

Pick a mode: Classic, Blitz (90s), Precision, or Weekly.

Weekly shares the same missions, bosses, and floor twists for everyone — you still build your own tower. Career XP, rank trails, and badges persist between runs.
